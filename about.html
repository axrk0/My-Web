About

The story so far

I'm a high school student who got into robotics through building with Arduino. It started with a simple blinking-light kit and turned into a habit of taking apart problems — how do I sense this, how do I move that — and wiring together a solution.

I mostly work on sensor-driven builds and small autonomous projects, and I've picked up CAD and 3D printing along the way to design and print my own parts instead of buying them.
